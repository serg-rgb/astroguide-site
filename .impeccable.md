# AstroGuide design context

## Users

Adults (18+) using astrology, Tarot and lunar prompts for calm personal
reflection and entertainment. Users may be emotionally vulnerable, so the UI
must remain supportive without implying certainty, diagnosis, guaranteed
predictions, or professional medical/legal/financial advice.

## Brand and tone

Mystical, intimate and premium; warm celestial darkness, restrained gold,
editorial typography and deliberate motion. Avoid neon, casino/gambling cues,
fear-based urgency, manipulative scarcity, and pseudoscientific authority.

## Product goals

- Make natal, forecast, Tarot, compatibility and lunar flows understandable.
- Keep AI involvement, personal-data transfer, entertainment limits and
  subscription terms explicit at the moment they matter.
- Preserve trust through truthful loading/error/deletion/entitlement states.
- Serve EN/RU/ES consistently and remain usable with assistive technology and
  enlarged text.

## Constraints

- Android is Jetpack Compose; the public site is static HTML/CSS/JS.
- Primary audience is mobile, including older Android devices.
- No third-party advertising or analytics SDK; first-party analytics is opt-in.
- Visual polish must never reduce contrast, touch targets, TalkBack semantics,
  localization resilience, or Play policy clarity.
